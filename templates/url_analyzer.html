What will be checked?

PhishGuard will inspect URL structure for indicators such as unusual hostname patterns, misleading subdomains, IP-address hosts, suspicious URL syntax, and other documented rules.

These indicators are signals, not proof of malicious activity. A low score cannot guarantee that a website is safe.
